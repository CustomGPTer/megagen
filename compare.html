What MegaGen does not do

MegaGen has no online play over the internet, no cloud save sync and no Google Play updates, and it needs a 64-bit phone with Android 10 or later. It plays Sega's consoles only.

Where they do more

MD.emu supports RAR archives and has years of polish on many devices. Lemuroid plays many more consoles, from Atari to PlayStation and Nintendo DS, syncs saves to the cloud and installs from Google Play. Both say they collect no data.

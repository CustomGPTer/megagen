MegaGen 1.0.0 is the first release: a private player for the Sega games you own.

- **Seven systems in one app:** Mega Drive and Genesis, Mega CD and Sega CD, 32X, Master System, Game Gear, SG-1000 and Pico, with Genesis Plus GX and PicoDrive.
- **Play page:** Continue playing opens on the exact moment you left, with a shelf for recent games, favourites and each system.
- **Saves:** nine slots with a picture of each moment, three automatic saves, rewind, 2× and run-ahead.
- **Controls:** Controller Studio with 14 finishes and four arrangements, the right pad for each game, six-button controllers, light gun and mouse by touch, Team Player and 4-Way Play.
- **Picture and sound:** Sharp, Smooth, Original, Composite blend and CRT; Game Gear frame and ghosting; Model 1 or Model 2 sound.
- **Mega CD:** discs, multi-disc playlists, MSU-MD soundtracks and Sonic & Knuckles lock-on.
- **Two phones:** play a friend over the same Wi-Fi or a hotspot, and send MegaGen to their phone.

No games are included. Android 10 or later, 64-bit phones.

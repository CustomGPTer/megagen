Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the MegaGen app for Android (package uk.jj.megagen) and this website. MegaGen is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • Your games, saves and settings stay on your phone. They are never uploaded to anyone.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • MegaGen goes online only when you ask: cover downloads you switch on, and Check for updates.

What stays on your phone

Games you add are copied into MegaGen's private storage, or linked where they are if you choose. Save states and their pictures, cartridge saves, BIOS and lock-on files, cheats, settings, controller layouts and play time are kept there too. Android's cloud backup is switched off for MegaGen, so none of this is copied to Google. A backup leaves the phone only when you save one, to the place you choose, with an optional password.

Two phones and the network

Two-phone play talks only to the other phone on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. The phones find each other on the local network and exchange the game's settings, the starting moment and each frame's button presses. When you send MegaGen to a friend by code, your phone serves a small download page on your Wi-Fi or hotspot while that screen is open, and stops when you close it.

Check for updates asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the newest MegaGen release when you tap it, and sends nothing about you or your games. Cover downloads, if you switch them on, fetch a game's box art from the libretro thumbnails collection on GitHub when you ask for it.

MegaGen does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can remove any game from its menu, keeping or deleting its saves. Uninstalling MegaGen deletes everything it holds, so save a backup first if you want to keep your progress.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands on your phone.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. The only script on it unlocks the download once the lawful-use box is ticked. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

MegaGen collects nothing from anyone. Younger users should ask a parent before adding games or switching on cover downloads.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 26 November 2026.
